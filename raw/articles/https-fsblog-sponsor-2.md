We build relationships.

The Knowledge Project works directly with companies. We’re selective about who we partner with because our listeners trust Shane’s recommendations, and we intend to keep it that way.

Every sponsor read is **written and performed by Shane**, tailored to the partner, and woven into the episode. We limit each show to**3–4 sponsors**.
