## 摘要

<p>If you want to change your life—like really, seriously, no bullshit, actually change your life—do this:</p>
<p>Step 1: Write down everything you care about.<br />
Step 2: Arrange the list from most important to least important.<br />
Step 3: Cross out everything but the top 3 things.<br />
Step 4: Re-organize everything you do around those three things. </p>
<p>People make this mistake of setting new goals every January 1st, but forget that goals are the path, not the destination. </p>
